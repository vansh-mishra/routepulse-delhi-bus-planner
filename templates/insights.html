METHODOLOGY

How these insights are calculated

Transactions are grouped by route and local timestamp hour. Passenger totals are summed from passenger_count, and fare value is summed from amount. Origin–destination pairs are grouped by route, start stop and end stop.

These aggregates help describe ticketed demand. They are not passenger occupancy snapshots, vehicle GPS traces or measured running times. The journey planner therefore uses published GTFS schedules for its current travel-time baseline.
